Why I'm Writing Here

August 10th 2026 - After my internship and before school start

I'm convinced, in a dozen years, human thought and work will be absolete compared to what machines will be able to do, in terms of productivity. My justification for wanting to work, for wanting to study hard, for wanting a good job, was to strive to make a grandious, beneficial impact. This was made me want to change my current ways. I want to be a leader, gain greater satisfaction in doing work, and deepen connection with other humans. Important context: I finished my GE Vernova internship 3 days ago and have been reflecting on, not the work at all, but the people whom I've met.

Perhaps it's ego, perhaps it's pride, and perhaps those two are definetely the same thing: I want to be a leader when I grow up. I want to ALWAYS know what to say when people turn to me, how to excite people towards a cause, and an important question I often ask myself is how do I get to that point? The best way to learn is the stories of great men, for life is nothing if not biographies. Importantly however, you also consider the real side and not just the fanatasized one that pulls you in. My ideal leader is Obama. He is the presidential embodiment of the enlightment, that is an appreciation of reason. More importantly conveying to other people reason. Publicly laying out a problem say all can understand all sides, so all sides can see that reason, for reason shouldn't be subjective. Writing essays about topics will help me convey ideas clearly, as if you can't understand my point I'm trying to make, it's half my fault. A good analogy is the best food is worth nothing if... you were blown up! #inspire

paragraph on greater satisfaction in doing work - I will truly believe in what I'm doing

paragraph on deepening connections wit other humans - Connecting with others will grow in popularity. With AI doing the work, humans will turn to idolizing traits and actions similar to the romantism age. Human connection is one of those things. - My connection with my partner will be stronger if we can discuss these deep things. Write makes you better at displaying your own points, in a manner such that the other person can absorb it, and reply with something that actually affects your belief in your topic.
