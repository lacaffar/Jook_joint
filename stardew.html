Grandpa left me a farm, and I have never been more at peace.

Wake up, water the crops, say hi to the town, fall down a mine shaft, go to bed, do it again. A game whose whole point is to slow down and tend to things.

How to farm (the journal)

  • E / SPACE on soil: till → plant → water
  • sleep in the farmhouse to grow watered crops
  • harvest 9 parsnips and something good happens
  • face the pond and cast a line - reel when it's calm, hold when it thrashes
  • the mine pays in geodes. Granny Fern pays in gossip. the pond pays in boots.

Crops I always plant

  • potatoes
  • strawberries
  • hot pepper
  • blueberries
  • pumpkins
  • corn
  • ★ starfruit

What I'd add to this farm next

  • Seasons. Right now it's always spring - four tilesets and a crop calendar would make sleeping actually cost something.
  • Give the villagers a schedule so Granny Fern isn't standing in the same square at midnight as at noon.
  • A shipping bin that settles overnight, so the morning has a number in it.
